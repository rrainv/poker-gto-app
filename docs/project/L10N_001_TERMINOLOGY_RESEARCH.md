# L10N-001 terminology research

Research date: August 13, 2026.

This report records the evidence used for Riverline's Russian and Hebrew house
style. It is a usage study, not a claim that every poker community uses one
universal spelling. Product labels favor the shortest well-supported form;
explanatory copy may introduce a synonym once when that improves clarity.

## Sources

Russian:

- **RU-GT** — [GipsyTeam: Термины покера](https://www.gipsyteam.ru/poker/terminy), a current education/glossary page covering actions, streets, hands, draws, positions, and common loanwords.
- **RU-GT-B** — [GipsyTeam: Текстура доски](https://www.gipsyteam.ru/poker/tekstura-doski), [Диапазоны](https://www.gipsyteam.ru/poker/diapazony), and [Фолд-эквити](https://www.gipsyteam.ru/poker/fold-ekvity), used for board, range, and strategy language.
- **RU-GTF** — public GipsyTeam forum discussions such as [Построение диапазона конбета](https://forum.gipsyteam.ru/index.php?viewtopic=155986), [Сайзинг](https://forum.gipsyteam.ru/index.php?viewtopic=141919), and [Как самостоятельно разбирать доски](https://forum.gipsyteam.ru/index.php?viewtopic=155256), used as a community-usage check rather than as sole authority.
- **RU-PR** — [Poker.ru: Полный словарь терминов покера и сленга](https://poker.ru/terminy-pokera-i-sleng/), a broad contemporary glossary with actions, combinations, draws, odds, positions, and software-facing jargon.

Hebrew:

- **HE-PP** — [Poker Place: מילון מונחי פוקר](https://poker-place.com/poker-terms-dictionary/), an Israeli glossary covering action buttons, streets, hands, draws, odds, and positions.
- **HE-PT** — [PokerTown: מילון המונחים בפוקר](https://pokertown.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%97%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%A8/), an Israeli education glossary that explicitly describes shared player language.
- **HE-YN** — [Ynet: מאול אין ועד שואו דאון](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-5754083,00.html), useful evidence of colloquial Israeli player vocabulary such as אאוטים, אול אין, בלוקר, טווח, פולד אקוויטי, פלאש דרואו, קול, רייז, and שואו דאון.
- **HE-7XL** — [7XL glossary](https://7xl.io/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%A8) and [PPPoker glossary](https://pppoker.games/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%92%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%9D-%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%A8/), used as secondary corroboration. Their prose quality varies, so they do not override stronger cross-source agreement.
- **HE-F** — public Israeli forum/search results were checked for community usage. Older FXP threads corroborate the same basic action vocabulary, but their age makes them supporting evidence only.

## L10N-001R product-decision annotations

The evidence table above remains the research record. The following explicit
user/product choices override a research preference in Riverline UI copy:

- **Russian range:** research supports both `диапазон` and `рендж`; Riverline
  uses `рендж` and natural inflections such as `ренджа` and `ренджей`.
- **Russian workspace:** English `Playbook` is displayed as `Разбор`.
- **Hebrew range:** research supports `טווח` and `ריינג'`; Riverline uses `ריינג'`.
- **Hebrew workspace:** English `Playbook` is displayed as `ניתוח`.
- **Hebrew pot language:** `קופה` and `סיכויי קופה` are mandatory; `פוט` and
  `פוט אודס` are rejected product forms.
- **Hebrew street/hand/texture:** Riverline uses `פרה-פלופ`, `טופ פייר`,
  `דו-צבעי`, and `חד-צבעי` by explicit product preference.

## Decisions where usage genuinely varies

- **Hebrew range:** both `טווח` and `ריינג'` occur. Riverline uses the user-approved `ריינג'` product form.
- **Hebrew draw:** `דרו`, `דרואו`, `ריצה`, and `משיכה` all occur. Riverline uses the compact poker loanword `דרו`; ordinary prose may say that a draw “completes” rather than inventing a formal noun.
- **Hebrew board texture:** evidence is thinner than for actions and hands. Riverline uses user-approved `דו-צבעי` and `חד-צבעי`, while retaining `פיירד` and `קונקטד`. These are house-style choices, not claims of universal spelling.
- **Pot odds:** Russian and Hebrew sources support both loanwords and native phrases. Riverline uses `шансы банка` and `סיכויי קופה` in UI prose because they are immediately readable; `pot odds` may appear once in explanatory copy.
- **Hero:** retained as a Riverline role token. `Villain` is rendered as `оппонент` / `יריב` in prose because that reads more naturally.
