Info & Glossary
Everything you need to know about Poker and GTO terminology.

The Basics

Texas Hold'em: Each player gets 2 hole cards. 5 community cards are dealt face up. Make the best 5-card hand.

Blinds: Forced bets made by the two players to the left of the dealer (Small Blind / Big Blind).

GTO Slang & Terms

Unopened: The pot hasn't been raised yet (just the big blind is out).

Facing Raise: Someone raised before you, and you must act (RFI).

Facing 3-Bet: The 3rd bet in a sequence. (Big blind = 1, RFI = 2, Re-raise = 3-Bet).

Facing 4-Bet: Someone re-raised the 3-Bet.

Facing Bet / Check (Postflop): Options you face after community cards are dealt.

Bet 33% / 66% / All-in: Sizing recommendations relative to the current pot size.

C-Bet (Continuation Bet): The preflop raiser continuing to bet on the flop.

SPR (Stack-to-Pot Ratio): Effective stack divided by the current pot size. Low SPR means you are committed to the hand.

Polarized: Betting a range consisting of very strong hands and bluffs, but very few medium-strength hands.

Limp: Just calling the big blind preflop instead of raising. (Generally discouraged in GTO play!).

How to Use
A guide to the app's features and controls.

App Controls

Table Size: Number of players dealt into the hand (2-10 max).

Stack Size: The effective stack size in Big Blinds. If you have 100bb and villain has 50bb, effective is 50bb.

Pot Before Action: The total amount of chips in the middle BEFORE you make your decision.

Facing Size: The exact size of the bet you are facing. If facing a 3bb raise, set this to 3bb.

Last Action: The sequence of events before it gets to you (e.g. Unopened means everyone folded to you).

How to Use this App

1. Set your Table: Use the left panel to configure game parameters (Stack, Players, Rake).

2. Input your Hand & Board: Click the card slots to select your hole cards and any community cards.

3. Set the Context: Use the controls above the matrix (Position, Last Action, Facing Size) to describe the exact spot you are in.

4. Get the Action: The 'Decision' tab will give you a bottom-line recommendation. The 'Full range chart' lets you explore how you should play every possible starting hand in this specific spot.

5. Postflop Fallbacks: If you input board cards but haven't loaded a custom solver tree, the app seamlessly falls back to a fast Monte Carlo simulation to estimate your equity and provide an action.
